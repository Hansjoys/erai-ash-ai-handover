# Public handoff exclusions

Excluded intentionally:

- personal videos and screenshots;
- saved games and user profiles;
- original Elden Ring files and proprietary archives;
- regulation.bin, LuaBND/DCX and other game-derived binary packages;
- DLLs, native modules, injected runtime artifacts;
- local logs, process IDs and machine-specific caches;
- SDK/runtime caches and dependency trees.

Use the redacted reports and source-level research as the public handoff. The complete original materials remain in the local <LOCAL_WORKSPACE> tree until the owner deletes them.
